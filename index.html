Maybe the kids have grown and gone, and instead of relief you feel a quiet emptiness you weren't expecting. Maybe you've lost someone, or your marriage has changed, or your body doesn't feel familiar anymore. Maybe you're carrying everyone else's weight — and quietly falling apart under it.

Whatever brought you here, I want you to know: what you're feeling makes sense. This season of life is genuinely hard, and you don't have to keep pushing through it alone.

What sets my approach apart is a deep personal commitment to my own healing journey — I know this terrain from the inside. I understand how closely our bodies and emotions are connected, and I use that understanding to help you feel better in both.

My approach is warm, honest, and completely judgment-free. There's no script here — just two people working together to help you rediscover who you are, what you want, and how to move forward with clarity and confidence.

I currently offer telehealth sessions across Nebraska and would love to connect. If you've been thinking about reaching out, let this be the nudge.

My Philosophy

You are not too far gone. You are not too much. You are not alone.

"This chapter of your life is not the end of your story. It might just be the most important one yet."

The anxiety, the sadness, the feeling that you've lost yourself somewhere along the way — these are not signs that something is wrong with you. They are signs that something important is asking for your attention.

I believe that the second half of life holds some of the most powerful opportunities for growth — if we have the right support to meet them. My role is not to fix you, because you are not broken. My role is to walk alongside you as you reconnect with your own wisdom, strength, and joy.

Together, we'll quiet the noise, untangle what's weighing on you, and help you step into this next chapter feeling like yourself again — maybe even more so.
